Travel days tracker

Track Days Spent in Each Country

A simple answer to a common travel question: how can I keep track of how many days I spend in each country?

Start with the year

WhereYear organizes your time by country or region and adds it up across the year. Instead of keeping separate notes after every trip, you can see a clear total for every place you have stayed.

Keep an eye on today

The current stay view shows where you are and the consecutive days so far. If you have a departure in mind, set the date and see how many days remain at a glance.

Use the calendar to fill in the story

Yearly totals are useful, but a calendar gives them context. Review confirmed stays as individual trips or scan the year day by day to remember when each place was part of it.
