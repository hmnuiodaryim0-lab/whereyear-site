Your Private Travel History

Keep a confirmed history of country and region stays that is easy to revisit and stays on your iPhone.

Trips you can look back on

WhereYear collects confirmed stays into a readable trip history. See the country or region, revisit the dates, and use the calendar view to understand how trips fit together across the year.

Private by default

Your history is stored locally. WhereYear has no account, no ads, and no analytics, and it does not store a continuous precise-location history as your travel history.

Keep a copy that works for you

Create a local backup for your records or export confirmed stay history as CSV. You remain in control of what you keep and where you share it.
