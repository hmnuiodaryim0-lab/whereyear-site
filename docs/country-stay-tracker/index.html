A Clear Record of Every Stay

Follow each stay from arrival through departure, with the days in between easy to understand.

Arrival to history

A stay has a natural shape: arrival, the current stay, days so far, departure, then history. WhereYear keeps those moments together so you can focus on the place instead of reconstructing dates later.

Plan without the paperwork

Add a planned departure date to see a straightforward countdown and optionally schedule a local reminder. It is a helpful travel-planning detail, not a legal or compliance tool.

Review stays when the trip is over

Once a stay is confirmed, it becomes part of your personal trip history and calendar. You can revisit it alongside other places from the year, all stored locally on your iPhone.
