Days abroad tracker

Keep Track of Days Abroad

For frequent travelers, people living across borders, and anyone spending an extended time away from home.

A year can cross many borders

Short visits, long stays, returns, and new destinations can blur together. WhereYear gives your time abroad a simple home: a country or region, a stay, and the days that belong to it.

Keep the record personal

Review the current stay while you travel, then confirm history when you are ready. There is no account to create, no advertising, and no analytics following your travel record.

Remember time, not routes

WhereYear is designed for a personal view of where your year went. Use its calendar and trip history to revisit time spent abroad, then make a local backup or export a CSV for your own files.
