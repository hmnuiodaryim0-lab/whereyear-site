Country day tracker

Know Where Your Days Go

WhereYear is a country day tracker for iPhone that gives each year a clear, personal timeline.

See every country in your year

Open WhereYear to see which countries or regions have shaped your year and the total days attached to each one. It is a simple way to keep a personal record without turning travel into a spreadsheet.

Understand your current stay

When you are away, WhereYear can show your current country or region and the consecutive days in this stay. Add a planned departure date when you want a calm reminder of the days remaining.

Look back at the whole year

Confirmed stays and the calendar view make it easy to revisit the places that filled your year. Your records remain on your device, with local backup and CSV export available when you need them.
