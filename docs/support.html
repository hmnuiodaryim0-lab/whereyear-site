Using WhereYear

WhereYear keeps your stay history on your device. You can review and correct confirmed stays in History, plan a departure in Today, and create a local backup or CSV export from Settings.

Location and reminders

WhereYear may use your device’s location permission to help identify your current country or region. Local notifications are optional and can be managed in iOS Settings.
